## Redrafting America

Redrafting America is a nonprofit civic initiative designing **Constitution v2.0**—a
modern constitutional framework for a future democratic society built around truth,
accountability, human dignity, and institutions worthy of public trust.

We are not proposing a collection of isolated amendments. We are asking a larger
question: **What should a constitution written today say, knowing everything the last
two and a half centuries have taught us?**

Our work begins from a simple conviction: democratic institutions should not depend on
tradition, personal restraint, or unwritten norms when fundamental rights and public
confidence are at stake. The rules that govern power should be clear enough to follow,
strong enough to withstand abuse, and open enough for the public to understand.

**[Visit redraftingamerica.org](https://redraftingamerica.org)**

## Constitution v2.0

Constitution v2.0 is being developed Article by Article as a complete constitutional
framework rather than a patchwork revision of the existing document.

The project includes:

- **The constitutional text** — a working manuscript defining rights, responsibilities,
  public institutions, democratic safeguards, and the lawful exercise of power.
- **The Lexicon** — a companion reference defining the institutions and concepts used
  throughout the framework, including the Concordium, Aequitium, Veritium, and others.
- **White Papers and explainers** — accessible accounts of major design choices, the
  problems they address, the alternatives considered, and the reasoning behind them.
- **A public drafting record** — versions, lifecycle states, and permanent identifiers
  that make it possible to determine what a document is, when it changed, and whether it
  was authoritative.

A project proposing constitutional text should be able to account for every word it
publishes. Traceability is therefore part of the substance of the work, not merely an
administrative detail.

## What we are building

Redrafting America is developing more than a manuscript. The initiative also requires
the public and organizational foundation needed to explain, test, preserve, and
eventually advance the work responsibly.

- **A public knowledge base** for Constitution v2.0, its institutions, sources, and
  supporting arguments.
- **The Drafting Room**, a continuing publication for essays, field notes, experiments,
  and open questions arising from the work.
- **Nonprofit infrastructure**, including governance documents, formation work, public
  communications, and the systems needed to operate transparently.
- **Future civic tools** that can support education, structured deliberation, and
  meaningful public participation when the project is ready for them.

## Where things stand

Redrafting America is in its pre-launch formation and drafting phase.

- The Bylaws have been drafted.
- The Constitution v2.0 manuscript is in active development.
- The 501(c)(3) application is in preparation.
- The public website and its initial educational and organizational structure are live.

Nothing should be treated as final merely because it appears in a repository or working
draft. Material intended for public reliance will identify its version and status.

## System Observatory

> Always useful screensavers. Never flying toasters.

System Observatory is a native macOS product in development that transforms idle
displays into useful, thoughtfully designed information surfaces. Its three complementary
experiences—**C-TOP**, **M-TOP**, and **L-TOP**—explore different ways a display can
remain informative without demanding attention.

System Observatory is being created by Redrafting America as a nonprofit-owned software
product. Its application and website repositories are maintained within the Redrafting
America GitHub organization, while product-specific source, testing, Help, design, and
release records remain governed within those repositories.

**[Visit systemobservatory.app](https://systemobservatory.app)**
